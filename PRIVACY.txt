4G1 LIVE PRIVACY NOTICE — VERSION 1.0.0-rc3

4G1 Live runs locally on the user's Windows computer. The application does not
include analytics, advertising, user accounts, cloud sync, or automatic data
upload. Vehicle telemetry is held in memory unless the user explicitly saves a
CSV file. Settings and diagnostic logs are stored under %LOCALAPPDATA%\4G1 Live.

The software communicates only with a locally installed J2534 vehicle interface
driver selected by the user. That third-party driver is governed by its vendor's
own terms and privacy practices.

Users can remove local application data by uninstalling 4G1 Live and deleting
the %LOCALAPPDATA%\4G1 Live folder. Diagnostic logs may include adapter version,
DLL path, selected vehicle profile, and error details; they do not intentionally
record names, contact details, VINs, or precise location.
