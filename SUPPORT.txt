4G1 LIVE SUPPORT

Before requesting support, confirm:
- Windows 10 or later
- a correctly installed 32-bit J2534 adapter driver
- ignition in RUN mode and the matching vehicle profile selected
- no other application currently owns the J2534 adapter

Contact: trm.racinglooms@gmail.com

4G1 Live is supported by a single developer. Support is best-effort: expect a
reply within 3 business days, and longer over race weekends and holidays. There
is no telephone support and no guaranteed resolution time.

When reporting an issue, include:
- the 4G1 Live version (shown next to About in the app header)
- your Windows version
- adapter model and firmware version
- vehicle model, engine, and ECU part number
- the exact steps that produced the issue

Useful files to attach:
  Diagnostic log:    %LOCALAPPDATA%\4G1 Live\Logs\4g1-live.log
  Session telemetry: %LOCALAPPDATA%\4G1 Live\Sessions\

Review both before sharing them and remove anything you do not wish to
disclose. Session CSVs contain vehicle telemetry from your car.
