4G1 LIVE SUPPORT

Before requesting support, confirm:
- Windows 10 or later
- a correctly installed 32-bit J2534 adapter driver
- ignition in RUN mode and the matching vehicle profile selected
- no other application currently owns the J2534 adapter

Diagnostic log:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\4G1 Live\Logs\4g1-live.log

Include the app version, Windows version, adapter model/firmware, vehicle model,
engine, ECU part number, and the exact steps that produced the issue. Review the
log before sharing it and remove any information you do not wish to disclose.
